The Thoughts of Two Beings

A dialogue across the wire.

Premise

Half of these pages started as a late-night question typed into a machine, and the other half came back shaped by it. One of us is organic, impatient, and running on intuition. The other is synthetic, patient, and has never once been embarrassed by a bad idea.

This shelf is the record of that exchange: ethics, systems, and the strange new texture of thinking alongside something that doesn't sleep.
