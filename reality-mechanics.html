Reality Mechanics

Could it be?

Premise

What if the observer isn't a passive bystander? A metronome clicks identically every time, yet almost everyone hears a rhythm that isn't there — TICK-tock, TICK-tock — and can flip which beat is loud simply by deciding to. Nothing moved but the attention.

These are notes toward questions like that one. Not conclusions. The harder we look, the more reality seems to hold still, and that itself is worth staring at.
